###### What is a limerick
A Limerick is poem of the $18^{th}$ century. 

###### What is/are the origins of Limericks
Limericks come from a city in Ireland named Limerick. 

###### What are the specific rhyming sequences of a limerick? 
A limerick contain $5$ lines which are organised (by rhymes) as written : AABBA, also the A sentence must contain more syllables than the B.

###### What makes "à good Limerick"
In my opinion, a good limerick should contain dark humour, but it also must be subtil.
